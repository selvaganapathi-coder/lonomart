# Product Positioning

**Lonomart — simple website publishing for small businesses and individuals.**

The product competes on speed, simplicity, templates, easy editing, and fast publishing rather than maximum customization.

## Initial target customers

- Local businesses
- Consultants
- Freelancers
- Small agencies
- Restaurants
- Clinics
- Tuition centers
- Salons
- Shops
- Service providers
- Personal brands
- Small institutions

## Product principle

The editor is a mechanism. The published website is the product outcome.
