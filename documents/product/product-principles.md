# Product Principles

1. Optimize for speed.
2. Keep the customer workflow understandable to non-technical users.
3. Prefer structured editing over a free-form design canvas.
4. Preserve security and tenant isolation in every customer-owned operation.
5. Prefer measured scalability over premature infrastructure.
6. Reuse established architecture before introducing new patterns.
7. Keep public website rendering separate from the management editor.
8. Do not expand the MVP silently.
